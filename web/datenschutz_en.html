1. Data controller

The party responsible for any data processing relating to the Auftritte app is:

Scoreware
Email: support@scoreware.com

2. What data is processed

Auftritte is a management app for speaking engagements. All the data you enter is stored exclusively locally on your device using Apple's native database framework (SwiftData).

The following information can be entered or imported in the app:

  • Engagement details (event name, date, location, title, theme, language, duration, fee, status)
  • Contact details of the client (first name, last name, email, phone number, organisation)
  • Pending tasks, notes, and the date of the original inquiry
  • Expected number of attendees

We, the provider, have no access to this data. No data is transmitted to any Scoreware server. There is no user account, no sign-in, and no registration.

3. iCloud synchronisation

If you are signed in to your Apple device with an Apple ID and have iCloud and iCloud Drive enabled, the data you enter in the app is automatically synchronised through iCloud across your devices (e.g. iPhone, iPad, and Mac).

This synchronisation happens directly between your device and the iCloud storage of your Apple account. The data is held in a private iCloud database tied to your Apple ID — Scoreware has no access to it.

Apple's privacy practices for iCloud are documented at apple.com/legal/privacy.

You can disable synchronisation at any time in your device settings under Apple ID → iCloud → Auftritte.

4. Device permissions

The app asks for access to two system features of your device — only when there is an actual need. Access is strictly local; no data is transmitted to Scoreware or any third party.

Contacts

When you use the "Import from Contacts" function, the selected contact's details from your device's address book (first name, last name, email, phone number) are copied into the relevant engagement once. There is no ongoing synchronisation. You can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

Calendar

You can optionally push an engagement as an event to your device's calendar. To do this, the app accesses the operating system's calendar database (EventKit). The entry remains exclusively in your personal calendar; no data is transferred to Scoreware.

5. Disclosure to third parties

Your data is not disclosed to any third party. In particular, your data is:

  • not sent to any advertising network,
  • not transmitted to any analytics provider,
  • not used for advertising purposes,
  • not sold, rented, or otherwise commercially exploited.

6. Tracking and analytics

The app contains no tracking, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising identifier. No usage statistics, crash reports, or telemetry data are sent to Scoreware.

If you have consented to the transmission of anonymous diagnostic and usage data to Apple in your device settings, Apple may collect such data on its own. That data is subject to Apple's privacy terms and is not available to Scoreware.

7. Data export and import

The app offers the following functions that allow you to actively exchange data between the app and other programs:

  • CSV export: Produces a file with your engagement data that can be reused (e.g. in Numbers, Excel, or an accounting program).
  • CSV import: Reads a previously exported or hand-crafted CSV file into the app.
  • PDF export: Produces a printable overview of all engagements.

These functions are used exclusively at your initiative. The files are produced locally on your device; what you do with them afterwards (sending by email, AirDrop, cloud storage, and so on) is entirely your responsibility.

8. Deleting your data

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Individual engagements can be deleted via swipe gestures or the context menu in the list.
  • All engagements at once can be removed via the menu "More → Delete all engagements". The action is guarded by a type-to-confirm prompt to prevent accidental triggering.
  • The entire app database is removed when you uninstall the app from your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, the data is also removed from your other devices on the next sync.

9. Your rights (GDPR)

Since Scoreware itself does not process any personal data about you, any claims for access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, or objection directed at us cannot be acted upon substantively — we simply do not hold your data.

You can view, edit, export, and delete your own data inside the app at any time (see sections 7 and 8).

For data Apple processes as part of your iCloud account, please reach out directly to Apple: privacy.apple.com.

10. Children's privacy

The app is aimed at working adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. Since no data is collected on the provider side at all, the question does not arise in practice.

11. Changes to this policy
