What Auftritte does for you

Six reasons why self-employed speakers and consultants manage their engagements with Auftritte.

→

A clear status workflow

From first inquiry through contract and delivery to a paid invoice — every engagement moves through defined stages with colour-coded states.

⌕

Find anything fast

Full-text search across event name, title, theme, client, location, and contact details. Sidebar filters separate upcoming, pending, and past engagements.

⊞

List or table

Switch between a compact list and a sortable table with one tap. Column widths can be adjusted individually and persist across launches.

⌑

Contacts and calendar built in

Pull a contact from your address book in one step. Push the event date to your calendar with one tap. Native iOS and macOS integration.

⌃

Fees under control

Record agreed fees in CHF. The statistics view shows at a glance which amounts are outstanding and which have been paid.

↻

Available everywhere

iCloud sync keeps your data current on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. CSV import brings existing data in, PDF export produces an overview for accounting.
